Xin Long (龙鑫)

I am an assistant professor in Economics at Audencia Business School in France. My research mainly focuses on deviations from Covered Interest Parity (CIP)—a measure of dollar liquidity condition for non-US borrowers. Specifically, I explore the spillover effect of dollar liquidity on trade flows of non-US firms and output of non-US countries, as well as the role of political risk in explaining CIP deviations. Before joining Audencia, I was an assistant professor at Özyeğin University in Istanbul, Türkiye.
